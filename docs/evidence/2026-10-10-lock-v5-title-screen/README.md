# Lock-v5 unlock title screen

Before and after from two production builds (before: `origin/main` at `4ce98a58`; after: this branch). Journey seals a PIN vault and reloads to the lock screen at 390, 1024, and 1280 px.

## Approved design (lock-v5 / PR #947 + #1012 wordmark)

- Hero **CipherWordmark** (`includeMark`, up to 90em) above the unlock card, not inside the brand row (#1012 plates, lock-v5.html fit).
- **CipherDial** (eleven rings on the column seam) idle on the gate; aligns on successful unlock.
- **VaultDoors** split animation after unlock; `unlockCeremonyStore` keeps the gate until doors finish.
- **Release notes** stay beside the card on wide layouts (#778); newest row opens on arrival; narrow stacks with rows collapsed for dial band (#947 ReleaseNotes).
- Unlock form, guest/join/reset roads, and vault picker unchanged in behavior (ADR 0090 / §5 guest).
