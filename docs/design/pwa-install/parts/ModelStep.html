Option C · Its own step

can install

cannot — no trace

Why

Built first, against the two-step ceremony that existed at the time: installing matters to everyone who opens the app, so give it a screen of its own and withhold that screen where the browser cannot install. It reads well on a phone — one thing to decide, the commitment fixed at the bottom.

Costs

The ceremony has since been cut to one screen and one question — no stepper, no counter, no skip, no back. Re-adding a stepper to carry one optional offer would undo that outright, and buy a heading with it. The rail would also grow a segment mid-ceremony, since Chromium decides the app is installable a moment after the page loads. Withdrawn for the section.
