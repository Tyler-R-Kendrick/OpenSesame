Option B · A strip above everything

Why

Cheapest to build and it reaches every screen, not just first run — one component mounted once in the shell, dismissible, and it leaves the one-question ceremony untouched.

Costs

This is the amber notice the first-run ceremony was built to delete, wearing a friendlier hat: a block of colour above the question the reader actually came to answer, competing with it for the top of the screen. Install is an offer, not a status, and an offer that follows you around is a nag — the reader learns to hit the × without reading, which is the one outcome that makes the app worse at the thing it is trying to do.
