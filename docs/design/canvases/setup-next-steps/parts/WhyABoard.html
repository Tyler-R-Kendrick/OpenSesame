Three more steps, without becoming a stepper again

Setup was cut down to one screen and one question, and the progress rail was drawn, built, and then removed as furniture. Adding optional steps risks putting it straight back. It does not have to: these are not steps of setup, they are things to do after it, and that difference is the whole design.
