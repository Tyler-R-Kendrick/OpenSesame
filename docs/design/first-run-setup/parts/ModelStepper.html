Option A · One question per screen

Why

One decision on screen at a time, and the commitment always in the same place at the bottom — reachable by thumb, and the same target on every step. A first-time operator never has to work out which of six controls is theirs.

Costs

Four screens to walk even when three are skips. Someone who only came to change the Identity API pays for the whole tour unless the rail lets them jump straight to a step.
