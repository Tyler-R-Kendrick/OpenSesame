The prompt is the switcher. who@vault:/ — the first segment names the account and opens this menu; the second names the open vault (personal here) and opens the vault list (ADR 0089). The profiles in the middle are the org profiles the menu always had; the three roads at the bottom are new.

A guest reads guest@personal:/. Its menu has no account to add to, so the exits are Sign in and Sign out — the latter ends the provisional session and locks.
