Identity and Host are not shown at all until something goes wrong with them — they were correct out of the box and asking twice never made them more correct.

1

Detect before you ask. Tailscale discovery, the shipped loopback defaults and the last successful pairing are all known values. Offer them as one-tap fills under the field instead of leaving an empty box and a placeholder.

2

No Save button for settings. Preferences already commit on change; endpoints commit on blur with a Saved chip beside the label. A Save button on a settings pane is a promise that something can be half-entered, and nothing here can.

3

One control shell. 40px tall, tinted until focus, with a leading icon that says which plane this is and a tail that holds the state chip or the reveal button. URLs and secrets set in the mono face; prose stays in the UI face.

4

Sentences beat label grids. Two selects with uppercase labels became one sentence you read once. The explanatory paragraph under it changes with the choice instead of covering every case at once.

5

Cut the field that proves nothing. Confirm-password exists because the value is masked. Show the value, offer to generate one, and the field has no job left.
