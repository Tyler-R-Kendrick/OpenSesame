Withdrawing a grant stops new reads at once. It does not un-read what was already opened: to make future versions unreadable, the item is re-keyed on its next change — a whole vault re-keys every item, which takes a while.

Grants expire on their own. Each one is on the same lifecycle feed as certificates and rotations, so the warnings — a week, a day, an hour, expired — arrive on your webhooks without this page being open.
